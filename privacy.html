The Appeal Lab Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how The Appeal Lab handles information when viewers, streamers, or broadcasters use the extension. The Appeal Lab is currently a lightweight Twitch panel extension prototype for audience prompts, reactions, pulse checks, and creator engagement.

1. Information We Collect

In its current prototype version, The Appeal Lab does not intentionally collect, sell, or share personal information from viewers. Basic interaction choices, such as button clicks or prompt responses, may be processed only to display extension functionality during the live experience.

2. Twitch Information

Because The Appeal Lab operates on Twitch, Twitch may provide certain technical context required for extensions to function, such as channel, extension, session, or user-related authorization information. The Appeal Lab uses such information only as needed to operate the extension and does not use it to sell personal data.

3. Interaction Data

The extension may process viewer interactions such as poll selections, reactions, prompt submissions, or challenge participation. This information is used to show live audience feedback, engagement signals, or creator-facing interaction results.

4. Cookies and Tracking

The Appeal Lab does not currently use advertising cookies, behavioral tracking pixels, or third-party analytics cookies. Future versions may add analytics or configuration features; this policy will be updated if that changes.

5. Data Sharing

The Appeal Lab does not sell personal information. Data may be shared only when required to operate the extension, comply with legal obligations, enforce policies, protect safety, or comply with Twitch platform requirements.

6. Data Retention

The current prototype does not maintain a long-term database of viewer personal information. If future versions store configuration, poll, or engagement history, retention practices will be disclosed in an updated version of this policy.

7. Children’s Privacy

The Appeal Lab is not directed to children under the age required by Twitch’s terms. Users should access the extension only in accordance with Twitch’s age requirements and policies.

8. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect the extension and its related assets. However, no online service can guarantee complete security.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as The Appeal Lab evolves. Any material changes will be reflected by updating this page and its effective date.
